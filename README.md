# 🌍 Northwind Sales Operations Dashboard — Tableau

An interactive Tableau dashboard covering order performance across regions, employees, and product categories for the classic Northwind dataset, spanning **July 2013 – June 2015**.

---

## 🎯 Project Objective

Give sales and operations leaders a single view to answer questions like:

- How much revenue are we generating, and how many orders are we fulfilling?
- Are we meeting on-time delivery targets?
- Which countries, categories, and products drive the most revenue?
- Which employees are the top sales performers?
- Where are shipping delays concentrated, and how severe are they?

---

## 🗂️ Dashboard Components

The workbook contains one dashboard built from **8 worksheets**.

| Worksheet | Type | What it shows |
|-----------|------|---------------|
| **KPI cards** | Cards | Revenue, orders, avg order value, customers, on-time delivery %, avg ship delay |
| **On-Time vs Late Deliveries** | Pie chart | Split of on-time vs. late orders |
| **Revenue by Country** | Map | Revenue concentration by customer country |
| **Top 10 Products** | Treemap | Revenue share of the top 10 products |
| **Revenue by Category** | Bar chart | Revenue for each product category |
| **Revenue Trend** | Line chart | Monthly revenue trend over the full date range |
| **Sales by Employee** | Bubble chart | Revenue generated by each sales employee |
| **Shipping Delay Distribution** | Bar chart | Order count by number of days delayed |

---

## 🎛️ Interactivity

- **Filters on the dashboard:** Date, Category, Country
- **Cross-filtering** so every chart and KPI card responds to the filters

---

## 🧮 Calculated Fields

| Field | Definition |
|-------|------------|
| **On-Time Delivery %** | `SUM(IF [ShipDelayDays] <= 0 THEN 1 ELSE 0 END) / COUNT([OrderID])` |
| **Avg Ship Delay** | `AVG([ShippedDate] - [RequiredDate])` |
| **Avg Order Value** | `SUM([Revenue]) / COUNTD([OrderID])` |
| **Ship Delay Bucket** | `IF [ShipDelayDays] < 0 THEN "Early" ELSE STR([ShipDelayDays]) END` |

**Data fields used:** OrderID, OrderDate, ShippedDate, RequiredDate, CustomerID, Country, EmployeeName, CategoryName, ProductName, Revenue.

## 🚀 How to Use

1. Download or clone this repository
2. Open `NORTHWIND_DASHBOARD.twb` in **Tableau Desktop** or **Tableau Public**
3. If Tableau asks for the data source, point it to the Northwind CSV/database the workbook was built on
4. Use the filters to explore the dashboard

## 💡 Key Insights

- **Headline numbers:** **$1.27M** in revenue across **830** orders, an average order value of **$587.37**, **91** customers, a **93.01%** on-time delivery rate, and an average ship delay of **8.5 days**.
- **Delivery performance:** 772 orders shipped on time vs. just 58 late — a strong on-time rate overall.
- **Top region:** Portugal drives the highest revenue concentration on the map, followed by Canada and Mexico.
- **Top category:** Beverages leads revenue at **$267.9K**, followed by Dairy Products ($234.5K).
- **Top products:** Côte de Blaye and Thüringer Rostbratwurst are the top two revenue-generating products.
- **Top employee:** Margaret Peacock leads sales at **$232,891**, followed by Janet Leverling ($202,813).
- **Shipping delays:** Delay counts peak around 6–9 days, with the single highest spike at 7 days (108 orders) — a clear target range for logistics improvement.
